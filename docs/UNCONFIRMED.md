# Unconfirmed

Open questions: what was not traced or confirmed in the game, and what the viewer's model does not explain yet.
Open items about puzzle-only mechanics, which the viewer's walk does not use, are in [Out of scope](#out-of-scope).

## The game's mechanics

- The GEM slot gather repeats the beam update without recomputing light power inside the loop; how that settles was not traced ([Promesst beams and powers](MOVEMENT.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)).
- The water implicit floor reads the sky's z0 for z ≥ `skyground_barrier`. Whether that leaves no water floor in the sky, as [Support](MOVEMENT.md#support) says, was not checked against the stored value.

## Progression and the viewer's model

- **The first way into the sky.** The ruby lift at (84, 73) can be fed from the level data ([Routes that need these mechanics](MOVEMENT.md#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)), but neither save has ridden it. The plan is one ordered search, not seen in a save: it leaves out the hero's steps inside the ENDPOINT beam, Wardrobe door edges on swap lines and a TELEPORT hero's own walk; the jumps are traced from the beam rules.
- **The east sky pads.** From the lift's landing the walk reaches the pads that reveal the east only through the ice gates of `thin_ice_rooms` (7, 79, 38) and `beam_jail_v3` (1, 79, 38). How the hero first enters `thin_ice_rooms` (17, 30, 38) and the `beam_jail_v3` pocket (x −5..0, y 72..83, z 38) is not known ([Routes that need these mechanics](MOVEMENT.md#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)).
- **The sky hub's south way.** The rules are checked, the sequence is inferred: the fenced Switch (−17, 144, 37) is pressed by a mirage from the magic Mirror of the ice gate (−23, 147, 37) moved into place, and the presser is reflected away while the hero stands in the Gate. Neither was simulated. How the Rover of the ice gate (−9, 146, 38) is boarded is not known ([Routes that need these mechanics](MOVEMENT.md#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)).
- **`laser_pointer` → `all_together_now`.** The landing into B1 is derived from the rules, not seen ([Second Endgame](PROGRESSION.md#second-endgame)). From B1 to A no way is known. In a two-object search (the vertical magic Mirror and the MIRRORABLE and SKIPPABLE Rock (−10, 144), neither entering water) the Mirror stays in x −11..−6, y 142..144, between the x −12 wall and the water, so (−13, 141), the only cell of A's column over a z 44 floor, is out of its reach; and no Rock can stand 3 west of the Mirror on x −7, so none is lifted onto B1 for the corner cup. Leads: a CLONE (the hero on the arms of two magic Mirrors appears at both, and a Guy in the cup holds up the cell above), TELEPORT lines at z 47, the two seam PROTECTION Wardrobes. The plans use the `laser_pointer` ice gate (0, 149, 46) instead.
- **The end3 room order** is inferred from gate unlockers and positions; that no room opens early comes from the viewer's walk on the level data, not the game ([Final Endgame](PROGRESSION.md#final-endgame)).
- **The first way into the underworld hub and the end3 rooms.** The hub's pads open only from the end3 pad (4, 121, −24) or from an active Low tower, which stands in the hub, and room A is reached only by the chute behind the hub Gate (−15, 147, −21), which only an OPEN bump opens ([Final Endgame](PROGRESSION.md#final-endgame)). No OPEN beam reaches it as placed. Open: whether a Mirror ridden by a goblin turns when the goblin turns; what follows a goblin's death (a dead body holds nothing up, so its Mirror would fall to the floor at z −22, out of the beam row, inferred); the goblin kill rules used are the documented ones, not traced for this field; how (62, 159, −21) and `chain_gang` are reached; why the field's pocket also holds 13 jetty cells. Also not re-checked: the east pit's fence edges.
- **The plans' access steps**: the way onto the sky after rank 1 and the way into the underworld after the sky are the open points above, not seen in a save ([The requirement graph](VIEWER.md#the-requirement-graph)).
- **Leaving `intro`.** Each arrival's walk to the Door (45, 47, 0) and `overworld:start` is derived from checked rules, not seen in a save: the default save has rank 0 and no ending ([In `intro`](PROGRESSION.md#in-intro)). That the rank-5 gate (59, 0, 2) alone adds only its own cell is inferred.
- **Other east leads.** The launch pocket (441 cells at z 0, x 58–146, y 12–51, 21 levels) has no known first entry. The closed ice gates on its edge are opened by levels inside it, apart from two that close pockets of their own, and no pad from the main reach reveals a pad in it. Its one dry way in on the walk is the fall north off the z 21 tops of the spinning-beams sky area, from (104, 22, 21) down the open column to (104, 23, 0) (inferred). That area is entered only from above, through its z 62 cells, and its first entry is open; lead: the MINOR pad (69, 149, 19) to its Marker at z 62 ([Teleporter pads and fast travel](MOVEMENT.md#teleporter-pads-and-fast-travel)), then a fall onto the z 21 tops, not traced. The pocket's MAJOR pads (133, 34) and (131, 45) are enabled, but their twins (76, 112, 89) and (75, 114, 89) start disabled in Groups that don't exist, the first enabled only by a twin whose world pad (148, 25, 0) sits inside, the second only by its own world pad. Ground leads not yet tested: 2-rock bridges north from y 61 into the Mirrorable Mirrors pocket (no rocks there); stacked Wardrobe skills (no skilled layer reaches the row-98 approach today).
- **Underworld magic-Mirror swaps.** TELEPORT swaps with the magic Mirrors (98, 79, −21) and (103, 79, −21) reach `bottleneck` (100, 81, −21), which the walk lacks; their ice gates were not checked.
- **The `slot_machine_v2` route** is a sequence inferred from checked rules, not seen in a save. The step back to (28, 148) must come while the stone still skips ([Routes that need these mechanics](MOVEMENT.md#routes-that-need-these-mechanics)). The viewer's model has no CHARM skip, so its switch press finds no object for the flavor-5548 Switch, and the notted Gates stay open ([Moves](VIEWER.md#moves)).
- **Skip-lane approximations**: the walk from the TELEPORT Wardrobe to the line is not checked for a door-in that would reset the skill, and the stone's pushes are single ([Skip lanes and the TELEPORT swap](VIEWER.md#skip-lanes-and-the-teleport-swap)).
- **Pockets behind drops and other Gates.** On the fresh save some unreached levels sit in small enclosed pockets whose only ways in are drops from the z 1 wall tops beside them, which pocket floods don't walk back ([Pockets, leads and why](VIEWER.md#pockets-leads-and-why)), or sit behind closed switch, glyph (flavor 9) and monster Gates. Each needs its own check.
- **Lily drifts**: the walk takes a river Lily to be at every cell of its drift at once ([Cells](VIEWER.md#cells)). On the default save every drift longer than one cell is a closed loop, where that matches the game. On a drift that ends, the walk neither checks that the hero reaches a cell before the Lily has passed it nor that he rides with the current.
- **The magic Mirror on a Lily** at (152, 12, 0): the Lily floats on still water, with no movement group and no drift, so it moves only when a skipping stone kicks it, and its Spawner reset puts it back. Its hops, and the unlock plans through them, hold unless a stone kicks it first; the stone lanes near it were not checked ([Magic-mirror hops](VIEWER.md#magic-mirror-hops)).
- **Map-room links**: whether the game needs the three links that need a mechanic ([Map rooms](WORLD.md#map-rooms)). The Rover ride for (45, 124, 78) → (49, 123, 78) was not simulated; it is the way out of the `crystal_rover_*` pocket, whose first entry is not known. Walls and water close that pocket: east of its room the water (surface −21) runs from x 23 to 30 to the main shore at x 31, where `mirror_crystal_rover` (30, 86) stands, and its inner ice gate, in a PULL Wardrobe's cell, is opened by `crystal_rover_bridge` inside. Leads: the `mirrorable` Rover and the magic Mirror held by ice gates in the z −22 channel south of the room, both freed by `mirror_crystal_rover` and reset by the shore Activator (29, 85, −21); the Rover's track would push the Mirror. Neither was simulated. The walk casts the beam of the light at (58, 114, 78) once FUGreenBeamRoom exists, from the room's Gem_Slot ([Beams](VIEWER.md#beams)); that was not seen in a save.

## Out of scope

Puzzle-only open items. The viewer's walk does not use these mechanics.

- Run speed: the hero step writes its positions 0.16 s / run speed after it closes, and run speed ramps from 1.0 toward 2.3. What sets the 2.3 target (queued input or held keys), and whether `Settings.running_speed_limit` caps it, was not traced ([A move](MOVEMENT.md#a-move)). One more 0.18 s duration in the power solver, probably the ENDPOINT jump's, was not identified.
- The magic-mirror teleport sets a 0.45 s timer for the mirror and mirage bookkeeping. Whether it delays the first skip after a mirror teleport was not traced ([Magic mirrors](MOVEMENT.md#magic-mirrors)).
- The prelude's clear Markers walk six item lists: heroes, Gems, Mirrors and Artifacts, one taken to be Lilies and one not identified (probably Rocks). They read no prelude flag on the items. The prelude-end state also gathers the still-frozen Freezers first; what that list is for (likely the `clear_non_prelude_items_inside_freezers` Markers) was not traced ([Winning and other inputs](SKILLS.md#winning-and-other-inputs)).
- The level-start pass that sets every existence Group to `exists_by_default` and sets DOING_PRELUDE is one branch of the load; the world flag that picks that branch was not identified ([Existence groups](ENTITIES.md#existence-groups)).
- The weight crush: the turn's fall list after a crush also skips entities with one more runtime flag, whose name is not known ([Pushing and pulling](MOVEMENT.md#pushing-and-pulling)).
- The soft Door break has a third source not identified ([Promesst beams and powers](MOVEMENT.md#promesst-beams-and-powers)).
- Whether a second SMASH move on one Rock in a charm move changes the outcome was not traced ([CHARM](SKILLS.md#charm)).
- The Torc's exact rule was not traced ([TRANSMUTE and the other skills](SKILLS.md#transmute-and-the-other-skills)).
- Whether a Tree made from a crystal gets a fall check was not traced ([TRANSMUTE and the other skills](SKILLS.md#transmute-and-the-other-skills)).
